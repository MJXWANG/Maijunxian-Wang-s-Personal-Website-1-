I work on video reasoning and the evaluation of vision-language models. I build benchmarks and run large-scale experiments to measure how these models reason about the visual world — and where they fail. 我研究视频推理与视觉语言模型的评测,通过构建基准、做大规模实验,衡量这些模型如何理解和推理视觉世界、又在哪里出错。

I'm also interested in Physical AI, world models, and making AI more trustworthy and interpretable. I'm part of VBVR-Pro, VBVR, and Grow AI. 我也关注 Physical AI、世界模型,以及让 AI 更可信、更可解释。我是 VBVR-Pro、 VBVR 与 Grow AI 的一员。
